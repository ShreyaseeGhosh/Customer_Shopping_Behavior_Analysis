Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis:


This end-to-end data analytics project examines 3,900 customer purchase records to identify shopping patterns, customer segments, revenue trends, product performance, and the effect of discounts and subscriptions on purchasing behaviour.

The workflow uses Python (Pandas) for data profiling, cleaning, validation, and feature engineering,
SQL Server for business analysis, and Power BI for interactive reporting and insight presentation.

Business Objective:

The project was developed to answer questions such as:

>Which product categories and items generate the most revenue?

>How does purchasing behavior differ across customer segments?

>Which seasons produce the highest sales?

>Do subscribers spend differently from non-subscribers?

>How do discounts and promotional codes affect purchasing patterns?

>Which products receive the highest customer ratings?

>Which payment methods and purchase frequencies are most common?
